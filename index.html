How the times are worked out

I took the pattern from BHHC's own emails and website, then compared every published time with Hebcal for Brighton. BHHC uses Hebcal's standard Brighton times for candle lighting, Shabbat ends and fast ends. Service start times are set relative to those.

Back-test against published times

Each rule was re-run on the dates where BHHC's real time is known. A difference of 0 means the rule gives the exact published minute.

Gaps to close

  • Weekday Mincha & Maariv after the clocks change. 5.30 pm appears on both the March and October sheets, when sunset was about 6.03 pm. By December sunset is about 3.53 pm, so BHHC may move Mincha earlier. Each Low row shows the likely alternative, about 15 minutes before sunset.
  • Shabbat Shacharit in winter. The only winter (GMT) example, 14 March, had Shacharit at 9.15 am with Twenty Minutes of Torah at 8.45 am. Every summer (BST) sheet shows 9.30 am. It may simply have stayed at 9.30 since the new Board came in.
  • Rosh Chodesh, Chanukah and bank holidays. No BHHC example exists yet. Weekday Shacharit is shown at the usual time, with an earlier alternative.
  • Five weekly Orders couldn't be read. The sheets for 29 Aug, 5 Sep, 26 Sep, 3 Oct and 10 Oct arrived as scanned PDFs. Typed text from them, or asking the office to send the Word file as they did on 12 Oct, would tighten the weekday rule.
